4 CDC DATA USE RESTRICTIONS

CDC requires you to agree before any data is requested. By using these data you will:
  • Use them for health statistical reporting and analysis only.
  • Not present or publish death counts of 9 or fewer, or rates based on them.
  • Make no attempt to learn the identity of any person or establishment in the data, and report any accidental discovery to NCHS.
Violations can lead to loss of access and legal penalties.
